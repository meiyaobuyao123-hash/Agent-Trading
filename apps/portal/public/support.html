FAQ

How do I change the language?

Go to Profile tab, tap Language Settings, and choose your preferred language (English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean).

What chains are supported?

We support Solana, BSC, Base, and Ethereum for market scanning and trading.

Is my wallet safe?

Yes. Private keys are stored locally on your device using iOS Keychain encryption. They are never sent to our servers.

How often is market data updated?

Hot tokens refresh every 30 seconds. New token signals update in real-time.
